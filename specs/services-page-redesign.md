# Services Page Redesign

## Goal

Redesign `/services` into a richer, more cinematic explanation of the current service offers while keeping the route, offer count, and pricing architecture unchanged.

## Scope

- Keep `/services` as one route.
- Keep the four current services:
  - Web Development & Launch
  - Video Editing
  - Video Production & Filming
  - Brand Content Growth
- Expand each service beyond card-level summaries with clearer context, likely deliverables, outcomes, and when a client should choose it.
- Use cinematic storytelling images that make each service feel tangible and premium.
- Align visual direction with the homepage: dark surfaces, copper accents, polished spacing, and glassy depth where useful.
- Add purposeful animation that supports storytelling, scanning, and section transitions.
- Preserve current pricing architecture and avoid introducing new pricing tiers, bundles, or checkout logic.

## Content Direction

- Present the page as one connected digital presence system, not a list of unrelated freelance services.
- Keep language plain, client-facing, and outcome-led.
- Make each service answer:
  - what it is
  - what the client gets
  - what business problem it solves
  - how it connects to the broader content, web, and growth system
- Avoid heavy technical language unless it directly clarifies client value.

## Experience Direction

- Use a cinematic page flow with a strong opening, image-led service sections, supporting details, and clear inquiry CTAs.
- Give each service enough space to feel considered, not just a compact card.
- Avoid fatiguing users with repeated list-heavy sections. Each service should show only the highest-signal process and output details before moving toward the next action.
- Use animation for reveal, emphasis, and continuity; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep mobile reading order simple: headline, visual, explanation, outcomes, CTA.
- Keep CTAs focused on starting an inquiry or quote request.

## Out Of Scope

- New routes.
- New services.
- Pricing model changes.
- App code changes in this documentation slice.
- Asset production or replacement in this documentation slice.
